History & Evolution

PROGRESSIVE ADJUSTMENT SDN. BHD. commenced operations in the Insurance Loss Adjusting industry in 1972 as a Sole Proprietorship known as MALAYSIAN SERVICE.

As one of the pioneer establishments in the Malaysian Loss Adjusting industry, the company was awarded with a Loss Adjusting license by the Malaysian Treasury (Department) for the practice.

In 1982, MALAYSIAN SERVICE was incorporated as PROGRESSIVE ADJUSTMENT SDN. BHD. with an Authorized Capital of RM 500,000.00 and Paid-up capital of RM 150,000.00.
